sigma {sigma_difference} R Documentation

A measure of difference between two sequences f and g (from different organisms or from different regions of the same genome)

Description

is the average absolute dinucleotide relative abundance difference calculated as<br /><br /><br /> sigma(f, g) = (1/16)*∑|pXY(f)-pXY(g)|<br /> <br /> <br /> where the sum extends over all dinucleotides (abbreviated sigma-differences).
