kobas.fisher_test {KOBAS} R Documentation

The Fisher exact test (greater / enrichment tail) of a 2x2 contingency table.

Description

Usage

kobas.fisher_test(a, b, c, d);

Arguments

a

the upper left cell: the members of the term in the foreground list. [as integer]

b

the upper right cell: the foreground members that are not in the term. [as integer]

c

the lower left cell: the term members that are not in the foreground. [as integer]

d

the lower right cell: the members that are neither in the term nor in the foreground. [as integer]

Details

Authors

gseakit

Value

the p-value.
